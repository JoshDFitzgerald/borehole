How to drill

  • Every prompt has a hidden list of answers, ranked by how often people give them.
  • You get one answer and 25 seconds. Your drill bores down from wherever you are, to the line your answer belongs to.
  • Common answers stop shallow. The single rarest answer on each list is the Diamond.
  • An answer that isn't on the list is a dry hole: the drill doesn't move.
  • Seven prompts make a hole. After that you can keep drilling in endless mode until you burn through three drill bits.

Digby the mole digs alongside you. Tap Digby if you need moral support. There are 0 prompts in the bank, and you won't see one twice until you've played through most of them.
